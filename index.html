About Popbros Industries

Six decades of quality, built on constant innovation

We manufacture premium quality braided, woven and knitted elastic and rigid tapes, cords and trims made from heat resistant rubber threads, and are pioneers in manufacturing latex-free braided elastics.

Our aim is the ultimate in customer satisfaction. We combine the latest machinery available in India with equipment imported from Europe, and keep upgrading our technology so customers receive the highest quality at a fair price.

  • Latex-free braided elastics
  • Heat resistant rubber threads
  • European & Indian machinery
  • Cost-competitive production
